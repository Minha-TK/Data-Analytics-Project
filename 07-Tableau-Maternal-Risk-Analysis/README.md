# Maternal Health Risk Analysis - Tableau (Final Tableau Project)

## Overview
Final Tableau project. Analyzed 42 maternal records to understand risk factors - age, weight, PCOS, previous complications, gestational weeks, nutrition and fetal growth. Built in Tableau Public.

## Key Insights
- Total Records: 42, Previous Pregnancies: 32, Gestational Weeks: 6, Previous Complications: 27
- Risk Level: 2 factors 13 patients (31% - highest), 0 factors 10, 1 factor 5
- Obesity/PCOS Peak: Age 27-28 (3 cases each)
- Previous Complications / Miscarriage Peak: Age 33 (12 cases - sharp peak)
- Fetal Growth Peak: Age 32 (6 cases), Age 28 (4 cases)
- Complications by Week: 9W is most dangerous - Anaemia 18, Hypertension 13, Thyroid 11
- Weight vs Conditions: 65kg 15 conditions highest, 45kg 11 conditions
- PCOS vs Miscarriage: PCOS YES 0.92 vs PCOS NO 0.62 - PCOS much higher risk
- Avg Conditions by Age: Age 24 and 26 = 5.0 highest, Age 28 = 2.14 lowest
- Nutrition & Growth by Weight: Fetal Growth peak at 55kg (7 cases), Nutritional Deficiencies peak at 45kg and 55kg (2 each)

## Dashboard
1. Maternal Risk Analysis - KPIs, Risk Level Overview, Age vs Obesity/PCOS, Age vs Complications/Miscarriage, Fetal Growth by Age, Complication Rates by Week, Weight vs Conditions, PCOS vs Miscarriage, Avg Conditions by Age, Nutrition & Growth
