# Healthcare Disease Prediction Analysis - Tableau

## Overview
My first Tableau project. Analyzed 1000 patients to predict disease patterns - Cancer, Diabetes, Heart Disease, Stroke, COPD, Alzheimer's, Kidney Disease. Built with Tableau Public.

## Key Insights
- Total Patients: 1000
- Total Cancer Cases: 100 (10%)
- Most patients have 2 diseases - 357 patients
- Disease by Blood Pressure: Normal 472, High 458, Low 390
- Smoking vs COPD: Non-Smokers 53 vs Smokers 48 - Smoking is not only factor
- Family History: Cancer YES 54 vs NO 46 - Family history increases risk
- Cancer Peaks: Age 40-45 (8 cases) and Age 75-80 (8 cases)
- Heart Disease: Female age 30 peak 41.67%, Male age 65 peak 38.23%
- BMI & Diabetes: Obese 75 (highest), Normal 56, Overweight 55
- Glucose & Exercise: Normal Glucose + No Exercise 15.1% lowest
- Alzheimer's: Highest Age 80-85 (14 cases)
- Kidney Disease: High BP + Diabetes 15.7% highest - dangerous combo
