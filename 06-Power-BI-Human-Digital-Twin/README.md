# Human Digital Twin Clinical Insights - Power BI Dashboard

## Overview
Interactive 2-page Power BI dashboard to analyze 5K Human Digital Twins - health risk, genetic risk, mental status, diet, allergy, medications, demographics, lifestyle, income and longevity across 10 countries.

## Key Insights
- Total Digital Twins: 5K
- Total Countries: 10
- Age Range: 18 to 90 years, Average BMI: 25.06
- Average Health Risk: 17.02
- Average Polygenic Risk: 53.63
- High Cancer Risk: 28.80%
- Average Longevity: 75.57 years
- Chronic Diseases: 11
- Heart Disease Risk: 28.88%, Stroke Risk: 34.87, Diabetes Risk: 30.88
- Average Income: 105K
- AI Mental Status: Good 610, Fair 150, Excellent 89
- Alzheimer's Genetic Risk: Moderate 433, High 245, Low 171
- Diet Types - Total 849: Balanced 274, High Protein 129, Mediterranean 126, Vegetarian 89, Low Carb 81, Keto 78, Vegan 49
- Allergy Profile: None 2666, Pollen 543, Dust 498, Seafood 426, Penicillin 368, Peanuts 349
- Top Medication: None, followed by Metformin, Amlodipine, Losartan, Telmisartan, Insulin
- Heart Rate: Normal 4.49K
- Smoking: Non-Smokers Avg Age 65.51, Smokers 63.19
- Gender: Female 50.97% (2.47K), Male 49.03% (2.38K)
- Location: Urban 55.2% (2.68K), Suburban 25.36% (1.23K), Rural 19.44% (0.94K)
- Highest Earning: Doctor 8.2M, Lawyer 7.0M, Freelancer 7.0M
- Countries: USA, Canada, Germany, UK, Pakistan, India, UAE, Singapore, Japan, Australia

## Dashboard Pages
1. Human Digital Twin Clinical Insights - KPIs, Mental Status, Genetic Risk, Diet, Allergy, Map, Medications
2. Demographics & Lifestyle - Heart Rate, Smoking, Gender, Age Group, City Type, Highest Earning Occupations

## What I Did
- Cleaned and transformed clinical data in Power Query
- Created DAX measures for Avg Health Risk, Avg Polygenic Risk, Cancer Risk %, Longevity, Heart & Stroke Risk, Diabetes Risk, Avg BMI
- Built KPI cards, bar charts, funnel chart, waterfall chart, world map visual, word cloud, donut charts, line/area charts
- Added slicers for Allergy, Diet Type, Country, Occupation, Heart Rate Category, City Type
