# Tele Health Body Map Insights - Power BI Dashboard

## Overview
Interactive 3-page Power BI dashboard to analyze Tele Health consultations, patient health conditions, demographics, satisfaction and diagnosis trends using a body-map visual.

## Key Insights
- Total Patients: 81
- Follow up Required: 46.91% | Follow up Not Required: 51.40%
- AVG Consultation Duration: 35 min
- AVG Patient Satisfaction: 2.9 / 5
- Age Range: 18 to 85 years
- Specialities: 5 - Cardiology, Dermatology, General Medicine, Pediatrics, Psychiatry
- Gender: Female 257 (51.4%), Male 243 (48.6%)
- Weekday Consultations: 366 vs Weekend: 134
- Satisfaction by Mode: Audio (108) > Chat (66) > Video (57)
- Prescription Given: Yes 233, No 267
- Conditions: Healthy, Stress, Viral Infection, Dermatitis, Hypertension, Migraine
- Highest Satisfaction: April (3.23), Dermatitis (3.18), Pediatrics (3.12)
- Lowest Satisfaction: January (2.63), Healthy (2.85)

## Dashboard Pages
1. Tele health overview - Body Map, KPIs, Satisfaction by Mode, Monthly Trends
2. Patient Demographics & Service Insights - Gender, Weekday/Weekend, Satisfaction by Speciality, Prescription Conversion
3. Patient Satisfaction Insights - Consultation Mode Trends, Monthly Satisfaction, Satisfaction by Diagnosis

## What I Did
- Cleaned data in Power Query
- Created DAX measures for Total Patients, Follow-up %, Avg Duration, Avg Satisfaction
- Built Body Map visual, pie chart, bar charts, waterfall chart, treemap, line/area charts
- Added slicers for Consultation Mode, Months, Diagnosis, Day of Week, Speciality, Patient Satisfaction
